How results are banded

Every assessment returns a probability and a band. Colour never carries meaning on its own - each band is always labelled.

  • Low - routine review
  • Moderate - discuss and reassess
  • High - consider referral

How it fits the visit

  1. Step one

    Patient completes the EPDS items on a tablet or beforehand.

  2. Step two

    You verify the clinical fields, most pre-populated from the record.

  3. Step three

    Risk, contributing factors and suggested actions save back to the record.
