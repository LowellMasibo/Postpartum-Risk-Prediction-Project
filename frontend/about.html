About this project

Postpartum depression affects a significant share of new mothers, and most cases go undetected. We built a screening tool that estimates risk from a short questionnaire and, unlike most prediction models, explains every result in terms a clinician can act on.

How the model works

dataset

Trained on

Public perinatal mental health datasets, documented in the model card.

checklist

Screening basis

Validated benchmark instruments, combined with clinical and psychosocial factors.

visibility

Explainability

Per-factor contributions on every result, so no prediction is a black box.

Our goals

  • flag

  • flag

Limitations and intended use

warning_amber

This tool estimates risk. It does not diagnose postpartum depression, and a low result does not mean nothing is wrong. It was developed and tested on public datasets that may not represent every population equally, and it has not been validated in clinical deployment. It is intended to inform a clinician's judgement, never to replace it.

This system provides decision support only and does not diagnose. It has not been validated in clinical deployment and must not replace clinical judgement or established escalation procedures.
