Privacy notice

What we collect

Questionnaire responses, clinical details entered by your care team, and a patient identifier. No names are stored in this system.

Who can see it

Only clinicians assigned to your care within your service. Every access is logged.

How long we keep it

Assessment records are retained in line with your service's health record retention policy.

Your choices

You can decline screening at any point, and ask your care team to correct or remove your records.
